# Hearth terms of use

Hearth is open-source software (MIT license) built as a prototype for the Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026.

**It is not a medical device, a monitoring service, or an emergency service.** Hearth records what a person says during a voice check-in, scores how concerned a family member might want to be, and sends notifications to the contacts you configure. It can miss things, misunderstand things, and fail to deliver notifications. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

**You are responsible for consent.** Only set Hearth up for a person who understands what it does and agrees to it. Only list contacts who agree to receive notifications.
